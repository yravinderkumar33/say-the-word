LINKEDIN

Less typing. More thinking.

I’m building Say the Word, an open-source dictation app for Mac.

Hold fn, speak naturally, and release. Your words appear where you were typing. Double-tap fn when you want to keep talking hands-free.

Speech recognition runs on your Mac. Optional cleanup runs locally through Ollama, removing fillers and repeated words. There’s no account or telemetry.

This is an early preview, and I’d love feedback from people who write all day: emails, notes, messages and prompts.

Built for Apple Silicon and macOS 14+.

Explore the project: https://github.com/yravinderkumar33/say-the-word


VIDEO DESCRIPTION / ACCESSIBILITY TEXT

Say the Word is a private dictation app for Mac. The animation shows holding the fn key while speaking, then releasing it to paste a message. Optional cleanup removes “um” and “uh” using Ollama on the same Mac. The real Privacy screen confirms that speech recognition runs locally. Pressing fn twice starts hands-free dictation; pressing fn again ends it. The ending invites viewers to explore the open-source early preview on GitHub.
